Rules for choosing the perfect rug for your room

Whether you live on your own or with a family, your living room matters. Keep these in mind:

  • Pick a rug that is the right size for the space.
  • Let its colour and pattern complement your existing décor.
  • Match the style, traditional or modern, to the room.
  • Check that the room shape and rug shape work together.
  • Remember that lighting changes how a rug looks.
  • Consider the material, and choose one you love.

Frequently asked questions

What is the difference between a rug and a carpet?

A carpet usually covers a whole floor or comes in large rolls. A rug is a finished piece that defines one area, such as under a coffee table or bed.

Do you deliver across Pakistan?

Yes. Message us on WhatsApp with your city and we will confirm delivery time and charges.

How do I choose the right rug size?

Aim for a rug large enough that at least the front legs of your sofa and chairs sit on it. This ties the seating area together.

Do you supply masjid carpets?

Yes. We supply masjid saf carpets and jaynamaz in dense, soft and durable weaves, in several colours.

How should I clean a Persian or Turkish rug?

Vacuum regularly without a rotating brush, blot spills with a dry cloth instead of scrubbing, and use professional washing for deep cleaning.
